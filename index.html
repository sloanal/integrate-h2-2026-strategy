05 · What this means
What does this mean

Same strategy,
re-weighted for the Leader.

To be clear: nearly everything on the roadmap stays exactly as planned. Data graduation, the agent, and the platform core all continue at full weight. Serving the Leader changes the order of just a few things.

↓ DEPRIORITIZED

Budgets

EVM and commercial budgeting slide to next year: a sequencing call, not an abandonment. The Leader isn't buying budget tooling yet.

↑ HIGHER PRIORITY

Reports

Executive reporting and brief-ready outputs move up: leaders buy visibility and outcomes, and need to report up without burning hours building briefs.

↑ HIGHER PRIORITY

Risk

Risk surfacing becomes first-class: risk flagged before it becomes slip is exactly the report-up that gets a leader promoted.

Everything else holds. Only the sequencing moves, the mission is unchanged.

Data Promotion & Demotion

Move data across
every boundary.

Ladders to → 🏛️ Gov (primary) · P3 seamless public-domain interaction · 🏢 Velocity & Integrity
Drag to orbit
Promotion: a data object graduates Commercial → JWICSDemotion: a synced copy flows back down

No other program-management tool moves data up and down the classification ladder as a product capability. Being on these networks at all is rarified. Enabling collaboration between them is the moat.

Every cross-domain item displays as a synced copy: its own identity, permissions, and history, plus a sync state to its counterpart. Sync is symmetric; visibility is not. Demotion never mutates the high-side source.

08 · Initiative 03
03
Core Capabilities & Platform

Become the brief.

Ladders to → 💵 Commercial PMF · 🏛️ Gov executive visibility · P1 · P2 70+ NPS · P3
Move to tilt
The executive milestone view assembles itselfLive data → brief-ready, always current

The buyer is now an executive whose team reconstructs status by hand: screenshots, rebuilt tables, manually assembled briefs. We give that work a home.

Data plane

The work itself: resources, people, budgets, locations. Stackable dimension panels under a shared timeline.

Lens plane

Filters, display settings, saved views: representations that shape how data is shown without changing it.

Work output

Where data leaves the app: documents, presentations, exports, agent-shaped artifacts. This is Compose.

The Rail · navigational backbone

A persistent icon rail with an adaptive secondary panel. It gives risk a first-class home alongside inbox and approvals, a Workspace of stackable dimensions, Compose for reporting, and an Agents home where chats and call recordings persist instead of vaporizing. It even unlocks a stripped-down mobile view as a breakpoint.

Underneath, three supports. An engagement flywheel (status gravity-well, websockets, notification bundling) keeps data fresh, since the agent's credibility depends on it. Permissions-as-architecture makes every surface consult the same permission truth. And a relentless UX burn-down takes MVP features from "exists" to "valuable."
